# Game design

## Pillars

1. **Readable immediately:** cyan movement, outlined squad rail, explicit AP pips, labeled actions, and deterministic feedback.
2. **Distinct Axie jobs:** Kibo absorbs pressure, Xia closes and bursts, Bing scouts and reaches exposed targets.
3. **A compact tactical story:** cross a shattered ruin, use lanes and cover, defeat Brute/Hunter/Spitter in roughly 5–10 minutes.

## Loop and balance

Each living Axie refreshes to 3 AP. Movement and basic attack cost 1; signature costs 2; guard costs 1 and reduces the next incoming damage by 40%. Kibo heals and guards, Xia strikes up to two cells, Bing fires a long shot. Cover reduces eligible damage by 4/8. Chimeras score lethal attacks implicitly through lowest-HP targeting, then approach with a deterministic cover bonus.

The 14×10 arena has two flanks, a broken middle, blockers, cover cells, crystal landmarks, and fog. Rendering is visually elevated while rules remain two-dimensional and reproducible.

## Screen flow

`MENU → SQUAD INTRO → PLAYER PHASE ⇄ ENEMY PHASE → VICTORY/DEFEAT → RETRY/MENU`

Tutorial steps are select, move, attack, end turn. Help remains available at all times.

# Round 2 vision

Round 2 turns the fixed squad into an owned-Axie tactics platform: server-side Axie lookup, gene-informed roles, body-part ability choices, squad composition, authored missions, enemy families, progression, and narrative. Ronin/wallet features enter only where they improve ownership and player value—not as an access gate. Later work adds additional maps, status interactions, richer AI, mobile/tablet UX, accessibility expansion, telemetry by consent, and approved audio/art packs.

The current adapter, data-driven unit definitions, deterministic rules, and static-client/server-boundary decision keep that expansion credible without burdening Round 1.

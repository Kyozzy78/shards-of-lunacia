# How to play

Defeat the three Chimeras. Each living Axie begins your phase with 3 AP and can act in any order.

1. Select Kibo, Xia, or Bing in the world or squad rail.
2. Cyan cells are legal destinations. Click one to move for 1 AP.
3. Choose Basic Attack or the signature, then click a visible legal Chimera.
4. Guard for 1 AP to reduce the next incoming hit by 40%.
5. End Turn when ready; remaining AP is discarded after confirmation.

Kibo is a durable lane holder, Xia a close striker, and Bing a long-range scout. Stone cover reduces damage. Unknown fog hides detail and enemies; explored ground shows remembered terrain but not current enemy locations.

Camera: `WASD`, wheel, `Q`/`E`; squad: `1`/`2`/`3`; center: `Space`; end: `Enter`; help: `H`/`?`; cancel: right click/`Escape`.
